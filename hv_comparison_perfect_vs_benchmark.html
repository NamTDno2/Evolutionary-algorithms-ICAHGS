Explanation:

  • Fair Comparison Method: Benchmark uses only top-N best solutions where N = number of solutions in Perfect result
    • This ensures fair comparison with equal solution counts
    • Removes bias from large benchmark Pareto fronts
  • Avg N: Average number of solutions used for comparison (same for both Perfect and Benchmark)
  • Perfect HV: Average hypervolume of PERFECT results (normalized [0-1], reference point [1.1, 1.1])
  • Gap HV (%): Percentage difference from benchmark (with equal N)
    • Formula: Gap = (HV_Perfect - HV_Benchmark_N) / HV_Benchmark_N × 100
    • Positive (+): Perfect produces larger HV (better)
    • Negative (−): Benchmark produces larger HV (worse)
  • +/=/−: Win/Tie/Loss counts
    • Win: HV_Perfect > 1.01 × HV_Benchmark
    • Tie: Difference < 1%
    • Loss: HV_Perfect < 0.99 × HV_Benchmark
  • Evaluation Strategy:
    • 20C, 200C: 25% of baseline evaluations
    • 50C, 100C: 50% of baseline evaluations
